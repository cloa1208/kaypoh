Singapore River Festival turns 10 · 11–20 Sep

Ten nights on the river,
and the best bits are free

  • Fri–Sun, 7.30–10.30pm: 8-minute projection across the Boat Quay shophouses, Kinrara Lane. Sound runs on Cinewav, so bring earphones.
  • Fri & Sat, 8pm: Samba Masala drumming from a bumboat, Clarke Quay Central to UOB Plaza. Same crew on foot at 9pm.
  • Nightly to 20 Sep, 7.30pm: Read Bridge answers to your hands — lights, music, a DJ projected into the trees.

Go: Havelock II B1-32 · Mon–Fri · book for dinner.

BookGroup

Go: 11 Dempsey Road · brunch 11am Sat and Sun.
