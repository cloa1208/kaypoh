The One Thing · Free
Singapore River Festival turns 10 · 11–20 Sep

Ten nights on the river, and the best bits are free

Fri–Sun, 7.30–10.30pm: 8-minute projection across the Boat Quay shophouses, Kinrara Lane. Sound runs on Cinewav, so bring earphones.

Fri & Sat, 8pm: Samba Masala drumming from a bumboat, Clarke Quay Central to UOB Plaza. Same crew on foot at 9pm.

Nightly to 20 Sep, 7.30pm: Read Bridge answers to your hands — lights, music, a DJ projected into the trees.

Eat This Week

Five picks · prices per person
KAYPOH SAYS / THE FIRST PICK
1. Kuro Kare
$16.90 nett lunch · sets under $22  ·  WALK-IN · WEEKDAYS ONLY

Order: Iberico pork tonkatsu, 36-hour black curry.

Why: The SMU canteen legend, rebuilt as an 18-seat yatai by two ex-Fleurette chefs.

Go: Havelock II B1-32 · Mon–Fri · book for dinner.

2. Casa Mori
$14++ weekend brunch  ·  BOOK · GROUP

Order: kalipoquetas — a curry puff rebuilt as a croqueta.

Why: Willin Low of mod-Sin fame, with José Alonso of Kulto.

Go: 11 Dempsey Road · brunch 11am Sat and Sun.

3. Assaggi
$58++ lunch · $88++ dinner  ·  BOOK · DATE NIGHT

Order: the Pasta Journey. Amatriciana comes in the pan.

Why: Buona Terra's team, minus the tablecloth. Set menus only.

Go: 77A Neil Road · Tue–Sat · Outram Park, 7 min.

4. Kimpson's Table
$15–18  ·  WALK-IN · UNDER $25

Order: samgyetang, $18. Half a chicken, five hours in ginseng.

Why: Chef Patrick Kim's first shop after a decade cooking privately.

Go: 134 Amoy Street · Telok Ayer · closed Sun.

5. Loulouca
$16 cake · $28–35 plate  ·  BOOK · LATE

Order: the Tiramisu, $28, with sobacha ice cream.

Why: Louisa Lim ran Odette's pastry for seven years.

Go: 6 Ann Siang Hill · Wed–Sun · plates 7–11pm.

Could not confirm on an official page

· Casa Mori's $14++ weekend brunch — casamori.sg lists no prices; figure from City Nomads and HungryGoWhere.

· Kimpson's Table prices and hours — no website exists, so the link is the restaurant's Instagram; $18 samgyetang and the Mon–Sat hours are from The Ranting Panda and Daniel Food Diary.

· Cloudmills hours — no website. AsiaOne says 11am–7.30pm daily; Daniel Food Diary and Time Out say 11am–3pm, Tue and Thu–Sun. I used the latter two and wrote "till sold out" rather than a hard closing time.

· Kali Kali — no website; link is the bakery's Instagram. $6.50 set price from Eatbook.

· Twilight: Flea & Feast — the organiser has no event page for this edition, so the link is the market's Instagram. Halls 401 & 403 and the 12–10pm hours come from Suntec's own listing.

· Common Rare's current ticket price — the official General Info page still shows only the expired early-bird table. $7 Fri / $9 weekend is from Suntec's listing and District Sixtyfive. Early bird closed 31 August.

· Xava Skybar's 3am close — not stated on the National Gallery page; from City Nomads.

· Happy Mondays, Ash and Rachael Yamagata — Foo Chow Hall has no event pages, so those link to the promoter (LAMC Presents) and the artist's own tour page.

· Singapore River Festival programme timings — srf.sg confirms the 11–20 Sep dates, the three zones and the 11 Sep launch at Read Bridge, but not the individual start times. Those are from Little Day Out, which is on our source list.
