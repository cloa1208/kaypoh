1
Tembusu
$18–34++ a dish
BookGarden
Order: squid ink claypot rice with abalone, $34. Otak-otak slider, $20.
Why: Ex-Les Amis chefs cooking Straits food in the Ginger Garden. New this month.
Go: 1 Cluny Road · Napier MRT · 8am–9pm, to 6pm Mon–Tue.
2
Sushi Ryujiro
lunch $228++ · dinner $328++
BookDate night
Order: the lunch omakase. Same counter, half the dinner bill.
Why: A Tokyo one-star's first Singapore counter, under his own name.
Go: Capitol Kempinski #01-88 · City Hall MRT.
3
Casa Lola
$15–48++
BookGroup
Order: carabinero paella, $48. Crab and uni croquettes, $16.
Why: Ex-Barrafina chef Juanjo Carrillo's bodega, named for his grandmother.
Go: 3 Boon Tat Street · Telok Ayer MRT.

WHUCHU
