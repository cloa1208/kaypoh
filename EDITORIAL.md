# Kaypoh — editorial brief

Kaypoh is a weekly, two-page Singapore newsletter for one reader and his friends. It goes out Thursday morning and covers Thursday to the following Wednesday.

**Tagline:** Where to go, what to eat, what to book. Sorted lah.

## Who reads it

- 90s kids, now working professionals. Fashionable, call themselves foodies, go out on weekends.
- They are busy. They want decisions, not lists. Every item must answer: why this, why now, how do I get in.
- They know Singapore. Never explain what Clarke Quay is. Do tell them which MRT exit.

## Voice

- Opinionated and warm. "Go once, weekday 3pm" beats "a popular new opening".
- One idea per line. No run-on sentences. No paragraph longer than two lines.
- Light Singlish is fine in the tagline and the sign-off. Not in the body copy.
- Say "skip" as readily as "go". A skip verdict is as useful as a recommendation.

## Curation rules

**The One Thing.** One headline pick for the week. Free or cheap, photogenic, happening this weekend. Three to four one-line bullets with day, time, place.

**This week in 10 seconds.** Three lines: Go, Eat, Book. A reader who stops here should still be able to act.

**Ticket Alarm.** Six rows max. Lead with anything a 90s kid grew up on (90s and 2000s acts, F1 concert headliners, Singapore icons like Kit Chan). Each row gets a status pill: On sale, Resale, This week, Moved. Verify every show is still on. Check the venue's own event page for cancellation or postponement notices before including it. Cancelled shows must not appear.

**The Weekend Plan.** One Saturday, four to five timed stops, one MRT hop or one Grab, the rest on foot. Two cost figures: full plan, and the cheap version. One line for Sunday.

**Eat This Week.** Five picks. Rules:
- Mix cuisines. Never more than two of the same cuisine.
- Prefer first outlets and chef-led openings over second outlets of existing chains.
- At least one under $25 per person. At least one date-night pick. At least one group or brunch pick.
- Each gets: price per person, two tags (Book / Walk-in / Queue, plus a vibe tag), an Order line, a Why line, a Go line with address or MRT.
- The featured pick is the one a foodie would screenshot.

**Drink and Coffee strips.** Two bars, two cafes or bakeries. One line each.

**Pop-ups and Drops.** Three photo tiles: one opening this week, one to buy early for next week, one seasonal. Include a fashion or design item every week (markets, sample sales, brand pop-ups, sneaker drops). Then three short lists: Also on, Last call (closing this Sunday), Next week.

**Not the Merlion.** One off-beat attraction, inside Also on. Free if possible.

**Hype Check.** One Go and one Skip. Two lines each: what it is, verdict.

## Links

- Every named venue, event and show is a link.
- Link only to the official page: the restaurant's own site, the venue's event page, the ticket seller, the organiser. Never a blog, listicle or news article.
- If no official page exists, link the venue's own Instagram. If neither exists, leave it unlinked.
- Open every link before sending. Drop or replace anything that 404s.

## Sources to check every week

Events: Time Out Singapore hotlist, Little Day Out, The Travel Intern monthly, Confirm Good monthly, SRF and Esplanade calendars, Gardens by the Bay events, VivoCity and Jewel what's-on, Invade markets, Design Orchard, Suntec and Expo event calendars.

Tickets: Ticketmaster SG (venue pages for Indoor Stadium, National Stadium, Star Theatre), Live Nation SG, SISTIC, BookMyShow SG, The Kallang events, Star PAC events, Bandwagon Asia.

Food: Eatbook new restaurants monthly, Daniel Food Diary new cafes monthly, City Nomads just-opened, HungryGoWhere new openings, Sassy Mama new restaurants, The Straits Times food, Time Out food and drink.

Fashion and design: Common Rare, Invade, Makers Market, Design Orchard, Dover Street Market SG, sneaker and streetwear release calendars, mall event pages.

## Verification before send

- Dates and times cross-checked against the organiser's page, not the listicle.
- Prices quoted with ++ where the venue charges service and GST on top.
- Any item that could not be confirmed on an official page gets "check before you go" in its line, or is cut.
- Note in the email to Calvin anything you could not verify, in one line each.

## Facts that stay true across issues

- Reader: Calvin. The routine prompt holds the delivery address. Friends receive forwards, not direct sends.
- Issue numbers increment by one each week. Issue 001 was Thu 10 Sep 2026.
- Design is fixed. Do not change the CSS. Change only the content inside the existing structure.
- Photos are hotlinked from the organiser or venue where possible, then from food media as a fallback, credited in the footer.
